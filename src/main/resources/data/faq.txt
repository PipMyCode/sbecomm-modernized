STORE POLICIES AND FAQ

1. Shipping Policy
We offer free standard shipping on all orders over $50. For orders under $50, a flat rate of $5.99 applies. 
Expedited shipping (2-day) is available for $15.99.
All orders are processed within 1-2 business days.

2. Return Policy
You can return any unused item within 30 days of receipt for a full refund. 
Please ensure the item is in its original packaging.
To initiate a return, go to your account dashboard and select 'Return Item' next to your order.
Refunds will be processed to the original method of payment within 5-7 business days after we receive the returned item.

3. Payment Methods
We accept Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover, and PayPal. 
We do not accept cryptocurrency or cash on delivery at this time.

4. Order Modifications
Orders cannot be modified once they have been shipped. 
If you need to change your shipping address or cancel your order, please do so within 1 hour of placing the order by contacting support or using the dashboard.

5. International Shipping
Currently, we only ship within the United States and Canada. International shipping to other countries is not supported.
